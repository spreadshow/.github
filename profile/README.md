# Spreadshow

An open-source workbook for working with data: a spreadsheet you can query with SQL, and slides that stay connected to the numbers behind them.

## What it does

You connect a data source (a CSV file, a database, a data lake), look at it in a spreadsheet-style grid, query it with SQL or a visual query builder, and turn the results into slides. Because the slides point to the data instead of holding a copy, they stay up to date, and the people reading them can filter and explore.

## Who it is for

Anyone who works with numbers and wants to understand them or show them to others: individuals, students, teachers, researchers, small organizations and teams.

## Status

Early and experimental. We are working out the architecture and a first small version. Nothing is stable yet, and feedback is very welcome.
